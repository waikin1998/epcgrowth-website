Creative & content design in Malaysia.

Advertising design, social campaign visuals and video creative for Malaysian businesses. Explore EPC Marketing work and prepare your creative brief.

Give your offer a clear visual direction

EPC Marketing creates advertising visuals and video creative for business campaigns. The starting point is your offer: what the customer should understand, why it matters and what they should do next. We connect design with the destination, whether that is a WhatsApp enquiry or a campaign landing page.

Creative formats to discuss

  • Offer-led advertising: make the service, promotion and next step easy to identify.
  • Concern-led visuals: explain the customer need without losing the brand's identity.
  • Social video: introduce an offer through a sequence of visuals, captions and a clear closing action.
  • Campaign adaptations: discuss the sizes, placements and versions needed before production starts.

What to prepare for a creative project

Share your logo, approved wording, service details, intended audience, publication channels and any existing photographs or footage. Confirm permission to use client images, testimonials and before-and-after material. Regulated or sensitive claims need appropriate review before publication.

Agree the scope before production

Discuss the number of concepts, deliverable sizes, video duration, revision rounds, source-file requirements and deadlines. Copywriting, filming, editing and advertising management should be distinguished in the agreed scope rather than assumed to be included.
