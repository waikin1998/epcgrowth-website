WHAT WE WORK ON

Good SEO starts
with the right foundations.

A practical scope built around your business, your website and the searches that matter to your customers.

01

Technical website health

Review crawl access, indexing signals, mobile usability and page performance. Prioritise the issues that make your site harder to discover or use.

02

Search intent & keywords

Understand how customers describe their needs. Map relevant searches to service pages instead of putting every keyword on the homepage.

03

On-page optimisation

Improve titles, descriptions, headings, internal links and page structure so each page has a clear purpose.

04

Content that answers

Plan and refine service pages, practical guides and FAQs that help people compare options and take the next step.

05

Local search visibility

Review business details, service areas and Google Business Profile information to support a consistent local presence.

06

Measurement & reporting

Track indexing, search queries, clicks and agreed enquiry actions. Use the findings to guide the next round of improvements.

SEO THAT CONNECTS WITH CUSTOMER SEARCHES

Be discovered for the services
your customers need.

Reach people searching for a service, a problem or a location — even before they know your business name.

01

Help Google read your website

Make important service information accessible through crawlable pages, clear headings and connected content. We check access and indexing signals so Google can understand what your business offers.

02

Target specific customer needs

Build a keyword plan around services, questions and locations. For a hair-care business, an example could be “hair loss treatment in Kuala Lumpur”. The target depends on the services you actually provide.

03

Create opportunities in AI search

Useful, reliable content and strong SEO foundations can support eligibility for AI Overview. We work on the information and website foundations that help search engines understand and present your business.
