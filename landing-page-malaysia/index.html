LANDING PAGE DESIGN MALAYSIA

One offer. One page. A clear next step.

A campaign landing page gives your visitor a focused journey from the ad they clicked to an enquiry. Here is how we structure a landing page.

Landing Page Set Up

1. Compelling Headline
State your main offer clearly. A focused headline helps visitors recognise that they have reached the right page and gives them a reason to keep reading.

2. Clear Subheadline
Explain who the offer is for and what it includes. This adds context to the headline and helps customers quickly understand your value.

3. Hero Image
Show a relevant product, service or outcome. A clear visual makes the offer easier to understand and supports the message in your advertisement.

4. Social Proof
Include genuine testimonials, approved examples or relevant credentials. Credible evidence helps answer doubts and builds confidence in your business.

5. Benefit-Driven Copy
Explain how your offer helps the customer, not just its features. Short, specific benefits make it easier to decide whether the service fits their needs.

6. Clear Call to Action
Use one obvious next step, such as Get a Quote or WhatsApp Us. A visible button reduces confusion and makes contacting you easier.

7. Simple Form Fields
Ask only for the information needed to respond. A short, easy-to-use form reduces effort and helps visitors complete their enquiry.

PRICING, DELIVERY & SUPPORT

Frequently asked questions

How much do advertising management and website setup cost?

Advertising management starts from RM500; advertising platform spend is charged separately. Website setup starts from RM500, with a starting package range of RM500–RM1,200 depending on scope. Domain registration and hosting are charged separately. Confirm the deliverables, billing period and renewal charges in your quotation before proceeding.

How long does it take to complete a website?

Website completion is expected within 2–7 days after the required materials are received and the scope is confirmed. Agree the delivery schedule and approval requirements before work begins.

Do I need a website if I already use Facebook and Instagram?

A website is recommended as a dedicated destination for advertising, where customers can understand your offer and contact you. With appropriate privacy information and consent where required, Meta Pixel can help measure website actions and support remarketing audiences and campaign optimisation. It does not guarantee precise targeting or sales.

Who owns the domain and website, and what happens if I stop renewing?

During the engagement, EPC Agency holds and manages the domain and website. If you stop renewing, domain ownership, website files and transferable management access will be fully transferred to you, not merely licensed for use. Domain and hosting charges remain separate; coordinate the handover and future hosting arrangements before the current services expire.

Will EPC update and maintain my website after launch?

During the active engagement, EPC provides website updates and maintenance once per month. The work covered is agreed in the project scope; this does not imply unlimited redesigns or continuous monitoring.
