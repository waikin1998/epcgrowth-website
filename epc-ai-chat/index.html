What is EPC AI Chat?

EPC AI Chat is a business communication product by EPC Creative Marketing. It brings AI-assisted replies, enquiry management and human handover into a shared WhatsApp workflow.

Can my team take over?

The workflow includes human handover. We agree which conversations should be escalated and who is responsible for reviewing and replying.

Do I use my own WhatsApp Business account?

Businesses connect and authorise their own WhatsApp Business account. Confirm the current onboarding requirements and access permissions before connecting.

What do I need to prepare?

Start with your service information, business hours, common questions, enquiry requirements and handover contacts. We use these to plan the agreed conversation flow.
