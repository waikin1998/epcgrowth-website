QUESTIONS BEFORE YOU START

EPC AI Chat FAQs

What is EPC AI Chat?

EPC AI Chat is a business communication product developed and operated by EPC Creative Marketing. Its product page explains AI-assisted replies, conversation management and human handover.

Is EPC AI Chat separate from EPC Marketing services?

It is an EPC product, while advertising and website projects are separate service scopes. Confirm which product access and marketing deliverables are included before agreeing a project.

Do I connect my own WhatsApp Business account?

Businesses authorize their own WhatsApp Business account for the platform. Confirm current onboarding requirements with EPC before connecting an account. Never share passwords in a public enquiry.

Can a human take over a conversation?

Human handover is part of the product description. Discuss the current workflow and access with EPC so your team understands when it needs to review and reply.

Where can I check privacy and account questions?

Review the product privacy information and data-deletion instructions, and contact EPC for account-specific questions. Do not send sensitive customer conversations through a general marketing enquiry.
