WHAT WE WORK ON

Six parts of a stronger campaign.

01

Keyword & search intent

Find the phrases customers use when they are ready to compare, enquire or buy. Group related searches and exclude irrelevant terms.

02

Ad copy & useful assets

Write clear headlines, offers and calls to action. Add appropriate links and business information to help customers choose their next step.

03

Campaign & location setup

Organise campaigns around services, locations and goals. Choose suitable campaign types and settings instead of using one setup for every business.

04

Traffic quality & budget review

Review search terms, locations and spend. Refine targeting and negative keywords to reduce irrelevant visits and focus the agreed budget.

05

Landing page alignment

Match the page to the ad: explain the service, answer common questions and make contact easy. A click should lead to a useful experience.

06

Conversion measurement & reporting

Set up agreed actions such as completed enquiry forms. Review measurement with appropriate consent and separate contact clicks from confirmed leads.

HOW GOOGLE SEARCH ADS WORK

From a search to an enquiry.

Paid advertising uses an ad auction — not the organic SEO indexing process.

  1. 1

    Customer searches

    A person enters a query related to a product or service.

  2. 2

    Eligibility & matching

    Keywords, targeting, budget and policy status affect whether an ad can compete.

  3. 3

    Google’s ad auction

    Bids, ad and landing page quality, context and other Ad Rank factors influence placement.

  4. 4

    Ad appears & earns a click

    An eligible ad may be shown. The customer chooses whether to visit.

  5. 5

    Landing page & action

    The visitor reads the offer, then may enquire, book or buy.

  6. 6

    Measure & refine

    Review agreed conversions and search terms to guide the next changes.

QUESTIONS BEFORE YOU START

Google Ads FAQs

How much should my business spend on Google Ads?

Google Ads management fee is RM1,200; advertising platform spend is charged separately. Website setup starts from RM500, with a starting package range of RM500–RM1,200 depending on scope. Domain registration and hosting are charged separately. Confirm the deliverables, billing period and renewal charges in your quotation before proceeding.

What is included in Google Ads management?

Discuss keyword planning, ad copy, campaign setup, destination alignment, measurement and review responsibilities. The exact deliverables and reporting schedule should be stated in the agreed scope.

Do I need a dedicated landing page?

Not always. An existing page may work if it clearly matches the offer and supports the next step. A dedicated page can help when the general website does not answer the campaign-specific questions.

Will paying for Google Ads improve my organic ranking?

Paid advertising and organic search are separate. Buying ads does not purchase an organic ranking. Website SEO needs its own content and technical work.

How should I measure campaign success?

Agree a qualified-enquiry definition and record outcomes over the same period. Report media costs separately from total project costs, and avoid treating a contact-button click as a completed sale.

Plan Google Ads around relevant searches

A search campaign works best when the keyword, advertisement and destination answer the same need. Someone looking for a local service needs to understand what you provide, where you work and how to contact you. Sending every search to a generic homepage can leave those questions unanswered.

Prepare a focused campaign brief

  • List the services or products you want to advertise and the areas you can serve.
  • Separate high-priority offers from work you do not provide.
  • Choose an enquiry destination and define a useful conversion action.
  • Agree the media budget and any separate management or creative costs.

Why search terms and negative keywords matter

The words people search may differ from the keywords selected in an account. Reviewing search terms can help identify irrelevant traffic and useful new themes. Negative keywords help exclude unsuitable searches. These reviews support relevance, but cannot guarantee a particular cost per enquiry or sales result.

Google Ads or Meta Ads?

Google Search Ads can capture an existing search need. Facebook and Instagram Ads can introduce a visual offer to an audience that is not actively searching at that moment. The right mix depends on the product, buying journey, creative assets and available budget.
