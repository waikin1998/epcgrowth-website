Google Ads Marketing

Explore service →
How much does Google Ads management cost in Malaysia?↓

EPC Google Ads management starts from RM1,200. The fee covers the agreed management work, not the money paid to Google to run your ads. Advertising spend is separate. We confirm campaign scope, billing period and any setup work in your quotation.

How much advertising budget should my business start with?↓

Start with your service area, business goal and what a qualified enquiry is worth to you. Keyword competition and cost per click affect how much traffic a budget can buy. We agree a test budget and review enquiries and cost per lead before recommending changes. There is no single budget that works for every industry.

How soon can Google Ads generate enquiries?↓

Ads can start reaching people once the campaign is eligible and approved. Enquiries are not guaranteed from the first day. We need enough useful campaign data to assess search terms, costs and lead quality. The offer, competition, landing page and your response to enquiries all affect results.

Why am I getting Google Ads clicks but no enquiries?↓

Clicks show interest, not a completed sale. Common areas to check include irrelevant searches, a confusing offer, slow mobile pages, broken forms and missing conversion tracking. We review search terms, negative keywords, ad copy and the landing page together, then use measured results to guide improvements.

Meta Ads Media Marketing

Explore service →
Should I use Facebook Ads, Instagram Ads or Google Ads?↓

Google Search Ads reach people actively searching for a product or service. Facebook Ads and Instagram Ads help introduce your offer while people browse content. The right choice depends on your audience, creative assets and goal. A business may use both, with separate budgets and tracking to understand what each channel contributes.

How much does Meta Ads management cost?↓

EPC Meta Ads management starts from RM500, with advertising spend charged separately. The final scope depends on the campaigns and creative support required. Confirm the management billing period, content production costs and deliverables in your quotation before starting.

Do I need a website to run Facebook or Instagram lead ads?↓

Not always. Depending on the campaign setup, people can contact you through messaging or submit an instant form without visiting a website. A landing page is useful when customers need more information before enquiring. We choose the enquiry route around your offer and how your team follows up with leads.

What does Meta Pixel do, and how does retargeting work?↓

Meta Pixel helps measure actions on your website, such as page visits or form submissions. These signals can support campaign optimisation and eligible retargeting audiences. Retargeting lets you reconnect with people who have already shown interest. Tracking needs appropriate privacy information and consent where required; browser restrictions and audience eligibility can limit what is measured or reached.

03

Website Development

Explore service →
How much does a website or landing page cost?↓

EPC website setup packages start in the RM500–RM1,200 range, depending on the agreed scope. Domain registration and hosting are charged separately. Extra pages, custom functionality or additional work may change the quotation. We confirm the deliverables and renewal costs before you proceed.

How long does it take to build my website?↓

A website within the agreed standard scope is expected to take 2–7 days after we receive the required content and confirm the brief. Your logo, images, service information and timely feedback help keep work on schedule. Larger or custom projects need a separately agreed timeline.

What is the difference between a landing page and a full website?↓

A landing page focuses on one offer and one next step, such as a WhatsApp enquiry or booking. A full website gives visitors more information across pages such as Home, About, Services and Contact. For advertising, a focused landing page can make the offer easier to understand; a company website supports broader research and trust.

Who owns the website, and do you provide maintenance?↓

EPC manages the domain and website during the engagement. If you stop renewing, domain ownership, website files and transferable management access will be fully transferred to you. During an active engagement, agreed website updates and maintenance are carried out once per month. Domain and hosting charges are separate; arrange the handover before services expire.

Content Creator & AI Video Executive

Explore service →
Can your filming team come to my business location?↓

Yes. EPC offers on-site filming for business content, subject to an agreed location and production schedule. We plan the message, shot list and people needed before the shoot. Confirm filming hours, travel, equipment and the number of edited videos in your quotation.

Should my business use a KOL actor or build the owner’s personal brand?↓

A founder-led video can explain your expertise and help viewers become familiar with the person behind the business. A KOL or actor may suit a campaign that needs a particular presentation style. Choose based on audience fit, budget and usage rights. Neither approach guarantees followers, enquiries or sales.

Can the videos be used for Facebook Ads, Instagram Reels and other campaigns?↓

Yes, when the agreed formats and usage rights cover those channels. We can plan versions for organic content and paid ads, with a clear opening, captions and a relevant call to action. Actor permissions, music licences, aspect ratios and platform requirements should be confirmed before production.

What is included in AI-assisted video production and editing?↓

The agreed scope can include concept planning, scripts, filming, editing, captions and AI-assisted production tasks. AI tools support the workflow; they do not replace checks for accuracy, brand suitability and permission to use assets. We confirm video length, deliverables, revisions and turnaround before work starts.
