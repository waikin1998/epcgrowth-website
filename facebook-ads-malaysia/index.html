QUESTIONS BEFORE YOU START

Facebook and Instagram Ads FAQs

How much should I budget for Facebook and Instagram Ads?

Advertising management starts from RM500; advertising platform spend is charged separately. Website setup starts from RM500, with a starting package range of RM500–RM1,200 depending on scope. Domain registration and hosting are charged separately. Confirm the deliverables, billing period and renewal charges in your quotation before proceeding.

Are Facebook Ads, Instagram Ads and Meta Ads different?

Facebook Ads and Instagram Ads describe advertising on those platforms; Meta Ads is the broader term. The appropriate placements and creative formats should be agreed for your offer.

Does EPC create the ad images and videos?

EPC offers advertising design and video creative. Confirm the number of concepts, sizes, footage requirements and revision rounds in your project scope. See the portfolio for examples, not promised performance.

Can customers enquire through WhatsApp?

A WhatsApp enquiry journey can be discussed as part of the campaign scope. Confirm the destination, response owner and how relevant conversations will be recorded before launch.

Why can ads get clicks but few useful enquiries?

Possible issues include an unclear offer, mismatched destination, insufficient information or slow follow-up. Review the complete enquiry journey and distinguish clicks from qualified conversations before deciding what to change.
