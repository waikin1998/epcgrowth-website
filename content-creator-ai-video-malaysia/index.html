BEFORE YOUR FIRST SHOOT

Frequently asked questions

Can you film at my business premises?

Yes. Share your location and the scenes you need. Travel arrangements, permissions, shoot duration and equipment requirements are confirmed in the quotation.

Do I need to be confident on camera?

You do not need to be a professional presenter. We can plan short talking points and provide on-camera direction, or discuss an actor or KOL-led format.

Can these videos be used in Facebook and Instagram Ads?

Yes, when the agreed talent, music and footage rights cover paid advertising. Ad setup, campaign management and platform spend are separate unless included in your quotation.

What does AI do in the process?

AI can assist with ideas, script drafts, subtitles and editing tasks. Final content is reviewed by people. Using a person’s likeness or voice requires permission.

What is included and how much does it cost?

The quotation depends on location, filming time, talent, number of videos, editing, revisions and usage rights. We confirm these before production; no fixed package is assumed.
