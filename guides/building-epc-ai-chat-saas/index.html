I Used AI to Build an AI Chatbot. Then Built the SaaS Around It.

By EPC Marketing · 4 November 2026 · 3 min read

The story behind EPC AI Chat: a founder-built SaaS connected to AI APIs, designed around real customer conversations.

From using AI to building with it

I wanted to go beyond using AI to write a reply. I wanted to build a product around the conversations businesses have with their customers. That idea became EPC AI Chat.

I built the SaaS myself and connected it to AI APIs. AI was part of how I developed the product, and it is part of the experience the product is designed to deliver. The result is my own product, shaped around a business problem I wanted to solve.

A chatbot is only one part of the product

A reply is a moment. A customer conversation has a whole journey: the first question, the details you need, the person who follows up and the next step. That journey is what makes building the SaaS around the AI worthwhile.

EPC AI Chat brings the idea of AI-assisted conversations into a broader workflow: common questions, organised enquiries, useful context and a way for a person to take over. The goal is to help a business continue the conversation with a clearer picture of what the customer needs.

The AI connection makes it possible. The workflow makes it useful.

Connecting AI APIs is part of the build. Deciding how the product should fit a business is another part entirely. What information should it use? What details should it ask for? When should the team step in?

Those are the questions I want the product to answer in practice. A customer asking about a service should get a useful next step. A question that needs a quotation or judgement should have a route to a person.

Built by the person behind the business

There is something powerful about taking an idea all the way into a product you built yourself. EPC AI Chat is that project for me: a SaaS I built, connected to AI and developed under EPC.

I can talk about it from both sides—as the person building the product and as someone thinking about how a business handles enquiries. That connection keeps the focus on what the conversation is supposed to achieve.

Your business information. Your team in control.

A useful setup starts with the information you approve: services, common questions and the details your team needs before following up. We then discuss the conversation flow and when a team member should take over.

Available features, WhatsApp onboarding and follow-up options depend on the agreed setup. The right starting point is a walkthrough of your enquiry process, so we can discuss what fits your business.

See what AI can become when you build around a real need

EPC AI Chat started with an idea and became a product I built myself. That is the part I am proud of: using AI to create something a business can explore and put into its own customer conversation journey.

Want to see how it could fit your business? Tell us how your team handles WhatsApp enquiries today. Let’s explore the workflow together.
