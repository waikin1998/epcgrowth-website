Meta Ads or Google Ads: where should you start?

Compare Meta Ads and Google Search Ads for your Malaysian business using customer intent, creative readiness, enquiry handling and a practical testing plan.

Short answer: start with your customer's buying journey and the assets you can support, not a universal claim that one platform is cheaper. This guide compares Meta advertising with Google Search campaigns, not every campaign type offered by Google.

When a search-led approach makes sense

If customers already describe your service clearly when they search, map those needs to a specific offer and useful destination. Ask whether you can answer their questions about scope, location and next steps. A broad keyword alone does not tell you whether the searcher wants to hire an agency, learn a skill or find a job.

When a visual offer is a better starting point

For a visual product or service, consider how a short video or image can explain the offer before someone actively searches for it. Prepare more than a logo and a generic slogan: demonstrate the offer, explain relevant conditions and make the next step clear. Discuss Facebook and Instagram placements as part of the creative brief.

Compare readiness, not just platform names

  • Offer: what is the specific reason to enquire?
  • Destination: is the page or conversation ready to answer the next question?
  • Assets: do you have approved visuals and clear copy?
  • Budget: can you fund media and the work required to test it?
  • Follow-up: who responds, qualifies and records outcomes?

A practical first-test plan

Select one primary goal, agree the scope and record a starting hypothesis. For example: people searching for a particular local service may need a focused page before contacting you. Alternatively, a visual campaign may need a clearer offer and an enquiry conversation. Decide what evidence would justify continuing, changing or stopping the test.

How to compare results fairly

Use the same definition of a qualified enquiry and the same reporting period. Separate media-only cost from total campaign cost. Record duplicates and irrelevant enquiries consistently. More clicks or cheaper messages do not automatically mean more customers.

Should you use both?

Only if the budget, creative and follow-up capacity support both. Starting with a narrower test can make decisions easier. Avoid splitting a limited budget across channels simply to say that every platform is covered.
