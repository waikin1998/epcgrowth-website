Facebook Advertising Malaysia: 5 Checks for Your 2027 Meta Ads Plan

By EPC Marketing · 3 November 2026 · 3 min read

Prepare your offer, creative, landing page and enquiry follow-up before launching Facebook and Instagram advertising.

2027 preparation guide · Written using information available on 3 November 2026. Platform features and guidance may change.

Build the plan before the creative

Facebook advertising in Malaysia should start with a specific offer and audience. This is a preparation checklist for 2027, not a prediction of future Meta features or advertising prices.

1. Define a useful customer enquiry

Decide which service you want to promote and what information makes an enquiry useful. Give the sales team a simple way to record whether a message is relevant.

2. Make the offer easy to understand

Explain what the customer receives, where it is available and any important conditions. Keep the Facebook Ads message consistent with your website or WhatsApp response.

3. Prepare different creative ideas

Plan a product demonstration, a clear offer and an answer to a common question. Use approved images or video. Compare distinct ideas instead of changing only a background colour.

4. Check the destination and measurement

Test the landing page, form and contact buttons on a phone. Agree which actions you will measure. Discuss consent and the appropriate tracking setup before collecting visitor information.

5. Organise follow-up and review

Prepare responses to pricing, availability and service questions. Review enquiry quality with the team. Meta Ads and Instagram advertising are part of a customer journey, not a replacement for good customer service.

Frequently asked questions

Is this only for Facebook Ads?

The planning checks also help with Instagram advertising, while the creative format and placement requirements should be checked before launch.

Should every visitor see the same offer?

Start with your audience and service. Test relevant messaging and avoid making claims that your business cannot support.
