5 Google Ads Strategies to Improve Conversions in 2027

By EPC Marketing · 3 November 2026 · 3 min read

A practical planning guide for Malaysian businesses: better goals, relevant searches, clearer landing pages and useful tests.

2027 preparation guide · Written using information available on 3 November 2026. Platform features and guidance may change.

Start with a business outcome

Before planning your 2027 campaigns, decide what a useful result means. A furniture showroom may want a booked visit. A service company may want an enquiry from someone in its service area. A click alone does not tell you either of those things.

Write down one primary goal and what makes a lead suitable. Ask your sales team which enquiries became real conversations. Use that definition when you review campaign results.

1. Check conversion tracking before spending more

Google explains that automated bidding uses conversion data. If the measured action is wrong, the system can optimise towards the wrong result. Test your form and thank-you journey before increasing spend.

Keep a WhatsApp button click separate from a completed sale. Check for repeated events and avoid counting the same lead twice. Where available, connect qualified leads back to the campaign with a suitable measurement setup.

2. Review the searches behind your clicks

Your chosen keyword and the phrase a customer actually types are not always identical. Review search terms to find irrelevant intent. Negative keywords can help exclude searches that do not fit your offer.

For a paid training business, a search about free worksheets may need a different decision from a search about course enrolment. Review the context before excluding a word that could also appear in a useful query.

3. Match the promise to the landing page

An ad for a specific service should lead to a page that explains that service. Visitors should not have to search your entire website to understand the offer. Keep the headline, location and next action consistent.

For a Malaysian business, state the areas you serve and any important conditions. Make the page comfortable to use on a phone. Show genuine examples and explain what happens after someone contacts you.

4. Choose bidding around a reliable goal

Google’s guidance connects Smart Bidding with the conversions and values you supply. Choose a goal you can measure, and check that its value reflects your business. Automation is not a substitute for good inputs.

Avoid changing every setting after one quiet day. Allow for the time between a click and an enquiry. Keep a simple record of budget, targeting and page changes so you can interpret the results.

5. Test one clear idea at a time

Build a test around a question: does a clearer price explanation bring better enquiries, or does a shorter form reduce drop-off? Change a defined part of the journey and record your starting point.

Compare lead quality as well as volume. A lower cost per lead is not useful if fewer leads fit the service. Agree a review period that suits your sales cycle and traffic, rather than promising a fixed uplift.

Your 2027 preparation checklist

Confirm your goal, test tracking, review search terms, check mobile pages and choose one experiment. Give each item an owner before the new campaign starts.

These are planning recommendations based on information available in November 2026, not announced 2027 platform changes or a guarantee of results.

Frequently asked questions

Will these strategies guarantee more sales?

No. Results also depend on your offer, competition, budget, landing page and sales follow-up.

Do I need a larger budget first?

Not necessarily. Check measurement and wasted traffic before deciding whether more spend is justified.
