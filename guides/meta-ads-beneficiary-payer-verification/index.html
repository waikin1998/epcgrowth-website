Meta Ads Beneficiary and Payer Verification: A Guide for Malaysian Advertisers

By Adrian · EPC Marketing · 22 November 2026 · 8 min read

Your Facebook ad asks you to verify a beneficiary, advertiser or payer. Should you select the client, the business or the agency? Understand the roles before following your account’s targeting-specific requirements.

Start here: the beneficiary is the entity being promoted; the payer funds the ads. The operator may be neither. Identity verification and ad approval are separate checks.

What do beneficiary and payer mean?

The beneficiary is the person or organization promoted by, and benefiting from, the ad. The payer (also written as payor) is the person or organization paying for it. They can be the same entity or different entities. The beneficiary is not the audience seeing the ad, and it is not necessarily the employee clicking Publish.

Start with three questions: Who is being promoted? Who pays for the advertising? Who only manages the campaign? An agency managing an account does not automatically become the beneficiary.

ExampleBeneficiaryPayer
A beauty business promotes its treatments and pays Meta directlyThe business providing the treatmentsThe actual person or company paying for the ads
A car business hires an agency but pays Meta directlyThe car business being promotedThe business paying Meta
An agency pays upfront and invoices its clientIdentify the business actually being promotedCheck the payment arrangement and the definition in that verification flow; do not decide solely from the operator or cardholder’s name

These examples help identify roles; they are not approval decisions from Meta. For groups, franchises or advance payments, confirm the real contractual and payment relationships against the account’s instructions.

Is verification required for ads targeting Malaysia?

A real Malaysian targeting notice: the screenshot shows “1 task is required” under Ad transparency. It states that this ad set will not deliver to Malaysia until the task is completed and verified advertiser and payer entities have been selected. For this ad set, follow Start task in the official interface.

This notice uses advertiser and payer. Not every verification flow displays a beneficiary field. Follow the definition of each field in your current task, rather than automatically treating the agency operator as the advertiser. The screenshot does not establish a single effective date for every Malaysian account.

The campaign score is 100 while verification still blocks delivery. A recommended-setup score does not mean verification is complete. Placement and WhatsApp Status notices in the same screenshot are separate matters.

Requirements cannot be determined only from where the advertiser lives. Targeting locations, ad categories and account-specific notices can all matter. Meta’s official technical documentation explicitly requires beneficiary and payer information for ads targeting the EU and associated territories. That regional rule should not be treated as an identical workflow for every Malaysian local campaign. [1]

Meta has also announced an expansion of advertiser verification. Take an official account task seriously, including for Malaysian targeting, but do not assume another account’s deadline or menu applies to yours. [2]

If you read a tutorial about Taiwan, first check whether you are targeting Taiwan and whether the corresponding task appears in your account. This guide does not present Taiwan’s menu path as a universal Malaysian process or give an unverified universal deadline.

Verification details, business verification and blue badges

  • Beneficiary and payer details: identify the entities behind the promotion and payment. Entering a name and completing an identity review may be separate steps.
  • Personal identity verification: confirms an individual’s identity through the relevant official process.
  • Business or organization verification: checks business identity, information or relationships. The entry point and accepted evidence depend on the flow.
  • Meta Verified or a blue badge: do not treat a badge as proof that every advertising verification task is complete. Check the specific task in the ad account.

Record the full task name, affected asset and status, rather than relying on a general statement that you have “already verified.”

Six steps after receiving a verification request

Understand the three areas in this example

  • Verifications: available verification tasks appear here; some locations may require separate verification. Use the appropriate Start verification button and follow its instructions.
  • Default advertiser and payer: sets entities to be autofilled when required. “No advertiser added” and “No payer added” mean no entity is selected here; they do not by themselves show that the account is banned.
  • European Union: a separate regional setting is displayed. Completing this area does not establish that a Malaysia-specific task is complete.

After setting defaults, return to the actual ad set and check the required location, verified identity and entity selection. The interface also explains that relevant advertiser and payer details may be displayed publicly in Meta’s Ad Library. Review the name and disclosure information before submitting.

  1. Open the official interface. Go directly to Ads Manager or Meta Business Suite and confirm the notice appears in the account. Do not send identity documents to an unknown person claiming to be support.
  2. Identify the affected assets. Check the ad account, Business Portfolio, Facebook Page, Instagram account and targeting countries. Make sure you selected the correct account.
  3. Read the task and deadline. Distinguish identity verification from entering beneficiary or payer information. Follow the deadline shown in the account.
  4. Select the real entity. Use the actual business and payment relationship to identify the person or organization. Names and addresses should match the relevant evidence.
  5. Submit the requested evidence. Upload only through the official flow and according to its checklist. Keep the submission date, status and reference number; do not publish private identity documents or complete account identifiers.
  6. Check the ad set afterwards. Confirm the appropriate entities are selected, then check verification, ad review and delivery status separately. One completed task does not mean every asset has updated.

After submission: what does “verified” tell you?

The green status shows that this identity is marked verified in this view. Linked verifications and authorizations are separate entries. The badge does not establish authorization for every ad account, and zero authorizations does not by itself mean identity verification failed.

Return to the original ad set and confirm its advertiser, payer and outstanding tasks. Identity verification success is not the same as approval of every ad. Menus can vary by language, account and product update; identify your verification flow before repeatedly creating accounts or resubmitting the same documents.

What should businesses and individuals prepare?

Organize the following information first. The actual upload requirements and accepted documents are determined by your Meta verification page.

  • The real person or business name and consistent contact details.
  • Relevant business registration information. Malaysian businesses can prepare the SSM documents appropriate to their entity, without assuming every document will be accepted.
  • If requested, evidence of address, phone number, business relationship or personal identity. Check document dates, clarity and consistency.
  • The client-agency authorization, service and payment arrangements, so the responsible people can identify the correct entities.

Business registration alone does not automatically authorize every industry or advertisement. Financial advertising in some countries can require separate identity or license checks. Check the rules for the relevant category and location. [3]

What if verification stalls or ads still cannot run?

SituationWhat to check first
You cannot find the verification entry pointCheck the account, permissions, targeting and affected asset. Your flow may differ from a tutorial.
Names or information do not matchCompare the registered name, trading name, address and evidence, then follow the stated rejection reason.
The review is still pendingCheck official instructions and notices, and keep the reference number. Do not trust guaranteed instant approval claims.
Verification is complete but publishing is blockedCheck ad-set entity selection and any separate policy, payment or asset restrictions.
The agency and client are unsure which entity to enterDocument the promotion, payment and advance-payment arrangements, then compare them with the flow’s definitions.

Verification does not guarantee ad approval or campaign results. Do not borrow identities, buy verified accounts or misrepresent an entity. If a review is needed, use the official support options available within the account and provide accurate information.

Will verified details become public?

Do not assume all submitted details remain private. Meta’s advertising transparency features can display payer or beneficiary information in applicable regions, ads or interfaces. The experience can differ by location. Read the public-disclosure explanation in the current flow, and let the client know which entity will appear. [4]

Frequently asked questions

Does an agency automatically become the beneficiary?

No. Start with who the ad actually promotes. Managing ads and earning a management fee are not the same as being the beneficiary. Determine the payer separately from the real arrangement and account instructions.

Should I upload documents if I only target Malaysia and see no task?

Do not upload through an unknown entry point. Check the official account for tasks and requirements linked to targeting and ad category; another region’s process may not apply.

How long does verification take?

There is no universal review time promised in this guide. Follow the timing and notifications in your task; requests for additional evidence can affect the process.

Does one completed business verification cover all future assets?

Do not assume so. Check each relevant business, Page, ad account and regional task rather than treating one asset’s status as a universal exemption.

How can EPC help?

EPC can help interpret account notices, clarify promotion and payment relationships, and review ad settings. The appropriate person or business submits identity evidence through the official flow. Meta makes the final review decision; approval is not guaranteed.
